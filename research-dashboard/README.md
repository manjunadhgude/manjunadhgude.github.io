# The Economics of Jobs — Research Dashboard

Static, searchable companion to the canonical Notion research notebook.

- Live page: https://manjunadhgude.github.io/research-dashboard/
- Canonical Notion notebook: https://app.notion.com/p/41641ff4c274815ea192d40fb62a858c
- Full Notion research text archive: [research-library.md](./research-library.md)
- Canonical structured records: [research-library.json](./research-library.json)

## What the dashboard contains

1. **Visual overview:** causal chain, economic charts, conceptual diagrams and evidence snapshots.
2. **Complete research text:** a synced Markdown mirror of the full Notion page content, including chapters, tables, source notes and canonical JSON code blocks.
3. **Canonical JSON library:** all structured research records in one JSON array, with a searchable record index and individual JSON viewer.
4. **Source metadata and caveats:** study designs, populations, time periods, definitions and limitations are preserved in the research archive.

The Markdown and JSON archives are versioned in this repository. The visualisations are an additional navigation and learning layer; they do not replace or shorten the underlying research. When the Notion notebook changes, refresh both archive files and verify their contents before marking the dashboard sync complete.

## Research integrity

Charts and diagrams distinguish measured statistics from conceptual frameworks. Every statistic should retain its source, population, period, denominator, method and limitations. Cross-country or sector-specific findings must not be presented as universal or as India-specific estimates unless the evidence supports that inference.

The portfolio homepage remains unchanged; this dashboard is hosted under its own directory.
