SASIGD 2026 · IEEE

TrustAnchor: Layer-2 Blockchain Provenance for Pharmaceutical Supply Chains

published research
01

Question

How can provenance be verified while reducing the burden of storing complete records directly on-chain?

Method

Layer-2 provenance architecture using blockchain-linked records and off-chain verification mechanisms.

Evidence

Documented figures include 0.770 ms median offline verification latency and 99.4% on-chain storage reduction.

Next question

How should provenance architectures balance verification speed, storage cost and operational complexity as the system scales?

ICCCT 2024 · IEEE

Predicting Threatened Habitats Using Logistic Regression

published research
02

Question

Can a supervised classification model be used to predict threatened habitats from the available environmental features?

Method

Logistic regression used as the predictive modeling approach, with standard preprocessing, training and evaluation.

Evidence

The published work reports 93.55% accuracy for the habitat prediction model.

Next question

How robust is the result under different feature subsets, class balance conditions and alternative models?

07 · ABOUT

Technical work, explained by a person.

A concise layer for anyone who wants context before diving back into the systems.

I studied Computer Science and Engineering at VIT-AP, with project work spanning reinforcement learning, computer vision, LLM fine-tuning, retrieval-augmented generation, and full-stack software engineering.

My recent work focuses on making AI systems dependable: a tool-using Qwen3-1.7B agent with safeguards for business operations, and an enterprise knowledge copilot that grounds answers in retrieved evidence and abstains when evidence is insufficient. My reinforcement-learning image editor explores language-conditioned visual actions.

The research side matters just as much. I have published work on pharmaceutical provenance and threatened-habitat prediction, and I keep the site evidence-led: a metric is shown with the thing it measures, and an open question is labelled as an open question.

I aim to make the work inspectable: through architecture diagrams, evaluation scope, known limitations and published research.
